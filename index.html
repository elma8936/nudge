What is Nudge?

Nudge is a retro-inspired physical device system that helps you start tasks and stay grounded throughout your day.

Instead of relying on distracting apps, Nudge gives you a simple, tangible way to plan your day, carry your intentions, and follow through calmly and intentionally.

  • See Nudge in Action

Features

Designed with simplicity at its core, Nudge separates planning and action into two calm, focused interfaces. Each part of the system removes unnecessary complexity so you can stay present with what matters.

Docking station

  • Set daily intention Start your day by choosing a simple intention. This helps set your mindset before planning tasks.
  • Set 3 daily tasks Create a focused list of three meaningful tasks for the day. Keeps planning simple and manageable.
  • Take notes Write down thoughts, reminders, or reflections as they come up. Helps you stay organized without mental overload.
  • Track progress Visually see what you’ve completed throughout the day.

Nudge keychain

  • Review daily intention Quickly revisit your intention throughout the day. Helps you stay grounded and aligned with your goal.
  • Task reminder Get gentle reminders of your three daily tasks. Keeps you focused without needing your phone.
  • Pomodero timer A built-in focus timer to help you work in short, productive sessions. Supports sustained attention without burnout.
  • Breathing exercises Guided box breathing to help you reset and calm your mind. Useful when you feel overwhelmed or stuck.

Who is Nudge for?

Nudge is designed for Gen Z and young adults who struggle with focus, attention regulation, or executive function. It supports people who benefit from external structure to help them start and complete tasks.

It is also for those who are drawn to intentional, tactile systems. People who want to step away from screen-based tools and reconnect with physical interaction in their daily routines.

Nudge helps build a healthier relationship with technology. It reduces dependency on all-in-one devices and limits digital distraction.

Instead of apps and notifications, it offers simple tools like task planning, timers, and breathing exercises. There is no noise from a smartphone or smartwatch.

At its core, Nudge is about mindful technology use. It brings back physical, nostalgic interfaces and supports a more intentional digital footprint. It encourages starting small, staying grounded, and moving through the day with clarity.

Process

Our team formed around a shared interest in creating a physical gadget that supports people in a positive way. We were especially interested in our generation’s addiction to phones and endless scrolling. Through many conversations, we explored how difficult these challenges can be, particularly for people with ADHD, where even completing basic tasks can feel overwhelming.

We wanted to build something people would actually use. Early on, we realized that addressing this problem meant avoiding apps or devices that already contribute to distraction.

We initially explored creating a smartwatch that could track health vitals and tasks, but after presenting our idea to experts and gathering feedback, we narrowed the scope of our project.

We shifted our focus toward building better habits and supporting task completion. This led us to define a few core features, including a breathing exercise, a Pomodoro timer, and task reminders.

We began development by selecting hardware and creating Figma designs for the UI. Work was divided based on each team member’s strengths, allowing us to develop the hardware, interface, functionality, and casing at the same time. We chose to use an ESP32-S3 Touch LCD for the display and 3D printed the initial casing.

Our first iteration included a basic UI, working functionality with physical buttons, and an initial casing design. From there, we conducted user testing and iterated on both the look and feel of the device. Based on this feedback, we made significant updates to the UI.

As we refined the enclosure, we realized the device had become too large to function comfortably as a watch. This prompted a major shift in direction.

We transitioned to designing a smaller gadget that could clip onto a keychain or backpack. Inspired by retro devices like a Tamagotchi, this new form felt more practical for daily use and resonated strongly with our target audience.

As we added finishing touches, we also developed a docking station. We maintained the retro design language and aimed to create something that feels natural to keep on a desk while remaining visually appealing.

Once both components were built, we focused on integrating them into a seamless system. We continued user testing, incorporated feedback, and refined the overall experience. Our final step was customizing the device and polishing the interaction design.

This process led to our final Nudge ecosystem. The docking station is designed to live on a desk or counter, serving as both a functional and aesthetic anchor. From there, users can set daily goals and sync them to the device. The portable gadget can be attached to everyday items, allowing users to track and complete tasks throughout the day.

Ultimately, we aimed to create something people would genuinely enjoy carrying, something that brings a small sense of satisfaction and joy to everyday productivity.
